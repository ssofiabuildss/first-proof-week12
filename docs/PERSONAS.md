# FIRST-PROOF — PERSONAS v1
**Research status:** Both profiles are synthetic design/test personas, not interviewed individuals. Update this version only from actual user evidence.

## Persona A — Daniela Ruiz, 21, candidate
- **Context:** Mexican university student applying to entry-level analyst internships while taking classes.
- **Need in her words (synthetic):** “I don't want to do the same spreadsheet assessment over and over, but I need to know whether recruiters would actually accept evidence from somewhere else.”
- **Current alternative:** completes each employer's requested assessment and saves personal screenshots or notes.
- **Willingness to pay hypothesis:** low or zero unless participating employers demonstrably waive a requirement; untested.
- **Quit trigger:** product declares her “verified” without checking actual skills; unclear privacy; no employer adoption.
- **Persona test task:** submit a fictional Excel assessment record, identify exactly what the status proves, retrieve it after saving.

## Persona B — Alex Moreno, 32, recruiter
- **Context:** screens early-career applicants at a mid-sized company with established baseline tests.
- **Need in their words (synthetic):** “I need evidence that matches our exact requirements; a generic badge isn't enough to skip our assessment.”
- **Current alternative:** administers employer-specific tests and manually checks candidate materials.
- **Willingness to pay hypothesis:** only if equivalence, quality, time saved and costs are demonstrated; untested.
- **Quit trigger:** vague verification status, no defined equivalence, claims of automatic screening substitution, untraceable evidence.
- **Persona test task:** interpret a saved record and state what employer decision remains unverified.
